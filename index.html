What we work on

Everything below has come through the workshop recently. Not on the list? Call and ask.

All services

  • Logbook and general servicing

    Engine oil and filter, air and cabin filters, fluid checks and a full safety inspection. Minor and major services.

  • Diagnostics

    Check engine light on, a strange noise or a leak? We trace it to the cause before the work starts.

  • Brakes

    Brake repairs and maintenance, checked as part of every service too.

  • Clutch and gearbox

    Clutch replacements, flywheel inspections, transmission and gearbox servicing.

  • Cooling and oil leaks

    Water pumps, thermostats, hoses, oil coolers and timing cover seals.

  • Roadworthy repairs

    Handed a list of defects? We fix what is on it, so your car is ready for its RWC.

  • European and performance

    Range Rover, Mercedes-AMG, VW Golf R, Nissan Skyline and Silvia have all been through the workshop recently.

  • Upgrades

    Head unit retrofits with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, reverse cameras and dash cams.
